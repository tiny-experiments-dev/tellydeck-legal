Contact Tiny Experiments

For TellyDeck support or privacy questions, email cs.tinyexperiments@gmail.com. For account deletion, use the subject TellyDeck account deletion and follow the mailbox-confirmation instructions.

Never send passwords, verification codes, password-reset or email-verification links, authentication/session tokens, raw purchase tokens, payment-card details or private playlist credentials. Please do not send identity documents; they are not needed for our ordinary deletion process.

Opening an email link prepares a message in your mail application; it does not send a request automatically. Support deletion requires an operator-initiated confirmation to the registered mailbox and your explicit consent, not just the initial message's From address.
