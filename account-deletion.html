Data and account deletion

How to remove local TellyDeck information and contact Tiny Experiments about information you sent to support.

Publisher: Tiny ExperimentsLast updated: 24 October 2026
No TellyDeck cloud account is created in the current release. Account registration, sign-in, device activation and Premium checkout are not enabled. There is no active TellyDeck account record to delete through the app or this page. We will update this resource before account services are enabled.
On this page
  • Request help or data deletion
  • Remove local information
  • Independent copies
  • Other accounts & subscriptions
  • Future account services

1. Contact us without reinstalling the app

For a privacy question, help removing local information, or deletion of support correspondence we hold, email cs.tinyexperiments@gmail.com with the subject TellyDeck data deletion.

Describe the request and, if it concerns an earlier support conversation, identify that conversation using information you can safely share. Sending from the same email address helps us verify the request. We may verify control of the relevant address or ask for clarification before deleting information.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, private playlist credentials, raw purchase tokens or full payment-card details. Do not use a public GitHub issue to submit personal information.

You do not need to reinstall TellyDeck to contact us. An email link only prepares a message in your mail application; it does not send it automatically. We review privacy requests and communicate any verification needs or legitimate reason information must be retained. See the retention section of our privacy policy.

2. Remove information stored on your TV

  1. Use the app’s available source/history controls to remove individual items where supported.
  2. To remove all app-private information, open your TV’s Settings → Apps → TellyDeck and choose Clear data / Clear storage, or uninstall TellyDeck. Labels vary by device.
Clearing app data is destructive. It normally removes saved app-private sources, favourites, preferences, caches and app-private recordings. Preserve any independent copies you want before proceeding. We cannot restore information erased from your TV through this website.

App information is separate from an Android/Google account. You do not need to delete your Google account to clear TellyDeck’s local data.

3. Copies that need separate management

Files in shared/removable storage, earlier exported backups, copies held by a document provider and Android-managed cloud/device-transfer backups may remain after app-data clearing or uninstall. Use the relevant file manager, provider or Android backup controls to manage them.

Clearing TellyDeck does not erase source-provider, streaming/CDN, guide, artwork, email or hosting-provider records. Those parties have their own processes and policies. See the local-information and backup sections of the privacy policy.

4. Other accounts and subscriptions

This release cannot create a TellyDeck account or complete Premium checkout. Your Google Play account and any IPTV/media-provider account are separate services. Removing app information or emailing Tiny Experiments does not delete those accounts or automatically cancel independently purchased subscriptions. Manage them with the relevant provider.

5. Before TellyDeck account services are enabled

We will update this page and the privacy policy before enabling account registration or purchases, with the actual account-deletion pathway, affected data, any justified retention and expected processing arrangements. This page does not advertise an operating Firebase deletion service or promise a future backend’s cleanup deadline.

Public support and privacy contact: cs.tinyexperiments@gmail.com.
