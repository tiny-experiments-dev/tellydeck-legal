Data and account deletion

Delete a TellyDeck email/password account, request help without reinstalling the app, and understand which records and independent copies remain.

Publisher: Tiny ExperimentsLast updated: 25 October 2026
Account-enabled builds: these instructions cover builds with email/password accounts. Accounts remain unavailable in older, unconfigured releases. Account deletion is separate from clearing your TV's local information. New Premium checkout is disabled, and during a limited early-access period verified accounts include Premium features at no cost; this early access is not a purchase.

1. Delete your account in TellyDeck

  1. Open TellyDeck's account area and choose the account-deletion action.
  2. Review the deletion warning and re-enter your account password in the app. Do not email your password to support.
  3. Choose the destructive confirmation to submit deletion. A recent password sign-in and a device-signed proof are required before the server can accept it.

Account deletion does not require a Premium purchase. If you cannot sign in, use the password-reset option yourself or use the support route below. Do not send reset links or verification codes to anyone.

If an error occurs before the server accepts closure, do not assume deletion completed. An accepted deletion means the account has been irreversibly closed; it does not mean every remaining cleanup step is already complete.

2. Request account deletion without reinstalling the app

Email cs.tinyexperiments@gmail.com with the subject TellyDeck account deletion. State that you want your TellyDeck account permanently deleted and identify the email address registered to that account. You do not need to install or reinstall TellyDeck.

Before acting, an operator will initiate a confirmation message to the registered mailbox and require a reply confirming both control of that mailbox and your explicit consent to delete the identified account. The From address on the initial request alone is not proof of account control. This is a human support process, not automatic deletion when an email is opened or sent.

If you cannot access the registered mailbox, explain that without sending credentials. Account recovery or further clarification may be needed; we cannot proceed with destructive deletion until account control and consent are established. A newly created account using the same email address is a different account and requires separate verification and consent.

Never send passwords, verification codes, password-reset or email-verification links, authentication/session tokens, raw purchase tokens, full payment-card details or private playlist credentials. Please do not send identity documents; they are not needed for our ordinary deletion process. Do not submit personal information in a public GitHub issue.

Support correspondence and private verification/case records are separate from the cloud account database and follow the retention schedule below. You may also request their deletion. See the support and retention sections of the privacy policy.

Opening an email link only prepares a message in your mail application; it does not send it automatically. We may contact you about verification, clarification or remaining cleanup needs.

3. What accepted account deletion does

The server first records account closure and clears the account's device entries and access grants. It does this before attempting Firebase Authentication deletion. Once closure is accepted, the closed account cannot resume account-service use.

It then attempts deletion of the Firebase Authentication user and cleanup of security proof records and remaining personal account/device data. Failed or partial cleanup is kept for retry. An accepted account-closure response means irreversible closure, not confirmation that every identity record has already been erased. Cleanup outages do not undo accepted closure.

Account deletion is not a remote wipe of your TVs, provider records, exported files or backups. Any previously issued offline authorisation cannot necessarily be recalled immediately. Do not rely on account deletion as instant invalidation of an offline device.

4. Records that remain after closure

Completed-account closure records: after deletion of your Firebase Authentication user and cleanup of account/device/proof records are complete, the remaining hashed account identifier and deletion marker are scheduled to expire 30 days after cleanup completion. They are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous data. Pending cleanup records do not expire on that schedule, so cleanup can be retried. Database expiry deletion is asynchronous and may occur after the scheduled date; this is not a guarantee of complete erasure within 30 days of your request.

Support correspondence and private verification/case records are manually deleted 90 days after the request is resolved, unless a specific legal or dispute-related need requires longer retention; any exception is documented. They are separate from the account database. You may also request deletion of these records.

Cloud operational log metadata has a configured 30-day retention period, while required cloud audit logs have a locked 400-day retention period. These cloud logs use a global storage location, not the selected account-database region. Account deletion does not immediately erase these separate logs. Email, authentication, hosting and other providers may also retain information under their policies or obligations. These periods do not guarantee complete erasure of every copy or US-only processing.

We will update the privacy and deletion notices before enabling new purchases, including separate retention arrangements for purchase-ownership records. The completed-account marker schedule is not a promise to erase other providers' records or cancel subscriptions.

5. Remove information stored on your TV

  1. Use the app's available source/history controls to remove individual items where supported.
  2. To remove all app-private information, open your TV's Settings → Apps → TellyDeck and choose Clear data / Clear storage, or uninstall TellyDeck. Labels vary by device.
Clearing app data is destructive. It normally removes saved app-private sources, favourites, preferences, caches and app-private recordings. Preserve independent copies you want before proceeding. We cannot restore information erased from your TV through this website.

Clearing app data or uninstalling does not by itself submit a cloud-account deletion request. Deleting a cloud account does not by itself clear all local source, playback or recording information. Your Android/Google account is separate; you do not need to delete it to clear TellyDeck's local data.

6. Copies that need separate management

Files in shared/removable storage, earlier exported backups, copies held by a document provider and Android-managed cloud/device-transfer backups may remain after account deletion, app-data clearing or uninstall. Use the relevant file manager, provider or Android backup controls to manage them.

Deleting or clearing TellyDeck does not erase source-provider, streaming/CDN, guide, artwork, email or hosting-provider records. Those parties have their own processes and policies. See the local-information and backup sections of the privacy policy.

7. Other accounts and subscriptions

New Premium checkout is disabled. Your Google Play account and any IPTV/media-provider account are separate services. Account deletion, app-data clearing, uninstalling or emailing Tiny Experiments does not automatically cancel subscriptions or delete those other accounts.

Premium features included with a verified account during the limited early-access period are free early access, not a subscription and not a purchase: they create no charges and no purchase records, so there is nothing to cancel in Google Play, and the deletion steps above are unaffected. The early access ends automatically no later than when new purchases are enabled; after that, those features will require a purchase.

Early access includes all of 31 December 2026 UTC and ends at 1 January 2027 at 00:00 UTC, or earlier when new purchases are enabled. It does not renew automatically or create charges.

If you have a subscription through Google Play, manage or cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions. Manage source-provider subscriptions directly with that provider.

Account deletion does not transfer purchase ownership to a different or recreated account.
